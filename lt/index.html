What We Do

Two trades. One standard of craft.

Most companies do one or the other. We design the landscape and build the hardscape — so your whole property works together.

Landscaping

From first sketch to weekly upkeep, we shape outdoor spaces that stay healthy and look intentional in every season.

  • Landscape DesignPlanting plans, grading, and layouts built around how you use your yard.
  • Lawn & Property MaintenanceMowing, edging, pruning, and bed care on a reliable schedule.
  • Seasonal CleanupsSpring and fall cleanups that reset the property for the season ahead.
  • Planting & Garden BedsShrubs, perennials, and mulched beds matched to your site and light.

Masonry & Hardscape

Natural stone and paver work installed on a proper base — built once, built correctly, built to outlast New England winters.

  • Stone PatiosBluestone, paver, and natural stone patios sized for real gatherings.
  • Walkways & StepsEntry walks and stone steps that handle frost, drainage, and daily use.
  • Retaining WallsStructural and decorative walls that hold grades and frame plantings.
  • Outdoor Living SpacesFire pits, seating walls, and finished spaces you'll actually use.
